# Signals and DTCs

Wheel signals: pressure (psi), temperature (C), sensor battery (V), signal quality (%), timestamp (s), valid flag.

DTCs: TPMS_000 no fault; TPMS_101 low pressure; TPMS_102 high pressure; TPMS_103 high temperature; TPMS_201 sensor timeout; TPMS_202 invalid data; TPMS_203 low battery; TPMS_204 poor signal.

These are project-defined IDs, not OEM identifiers.
